Data Privacy Notice

This notice explains how the LGU Daet Scholarship System processes the personal data you provide in this application.

What data may be collected

Identification details, contact information, family background, education details, uploaded documents, and other information required to evaluate your scholarship application.

Why your data is used

Your data may be used to receive, validate, review, verify, communicate, rank, and decide on your scholarship application, and to maintain official records and audit trails.

Who may access it

Access is limited to authorized scholarship personnel, evaluators, and system administrators who need the information to process your application and manage official scholarship records.

How it is protected

Reasonable administrative, organizational, and technical safeguards are applied to protect your information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss, subject to applicable law and official policy.

Your acknowledgment

By continuing with this application, you confirm that you understand this notice and consent to the lawful processing of your data for legitimate scholarship administration purposes.
